# Changelog

Pitlane HQ is in **beta**. The phone apps share their version number with Pitlane HQ Agent and the
web (`version.txt`), `0.MINOR.PATCH`. A MINOR version brings a set of new features, and fixes go in as
"Fixes and improvements". 1.0.0 comes when the beta ends. Newest first.

## 0.9.1 beta

- The new layout now has its full look on every screen, with new fonts, cards, switches and tab bar.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.9.0 beta

- Turn on notifications in Account to hear when another driver beats your record and to get a summary of your week.
- Share a picture of your lap from any leaderboard you are on.
- Send the app's logs to us from the support chat when something goes wrong.
- The tabs are called Telemetry and Account, like on the PC.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.8.1 beta

- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.8.0 beta

- A new layout with a Tracks tab. Every circuit you drove opens its own page with your laps, sessions, races, the leaderboard and your notes.
- The coach and your laps open from the track you pick.
- Tap a licence on Home to see its iRating over your last races.
- Recent sessions show a Race, Qualifying or Practice badge and your fastest lap.
- A Le Mans Ultimate race summary shows the drivers and your best lap.
- A small clock after a driver's name says they have a penalty or a slowdown to serve.
- Recent records, the track page and the leaderboards show the game of each record.
- Maps show the track's own layout, with no lines drawn over it.
- A support chat on the button at the bottom right. Write to us and we answer there.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.7.2 beta

- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.7.1 beta

- Le Mans Ultimate leaderboards show the whole class too, with each driver's car.
- Laps driven in the rain or on a wet or damp track carry a badge on the leaderboards.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.7.0 beta

- Signing in with Steam or Discord no longer asks for your password.
- The lap analysis has a braking table of lap A against lap B, with the gear, the time lost and what to do.
- The lap list shows S1, S2 and S3.
- The coach's plan goes to the coach overlay on your PC with one button.
- The lap analysis and the coach give the same corners, tips and plan as the PC and the web.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.6.1 beta

- The lap analysis and the coach say when a lap was driven in traffic, on cold tyres or on a wet track.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.6.0 beta

- Le Mans Ultimate is here in beta. Its sessions and races carry an LMU tag and show contacts instead of iRating.
- Choose the game you look at in Home, Analysis and Community. The game your PC is reading live has a dot.
- Sign in with Steam or Discord, and link them in Account.
- The app speaks Portuguese (Brazil) too, beside English and Spanish.
- In Le Mans Ultimate, Home shows your Safety Rating and Driver Rank where the licences go.
- Leagues for Le Mans Ultimate too.
- Le Mans Ultimate shows its classes, and every session shows your place in it.
- Your phone shows the same numbers as the PC and the web.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.5.0 beta

- The coach tells you first the habits you repeat across tracks, you can rate every tip with 👍 or 👎 and under it you
  see where the fast drivers brake and how fast they take the apex.
- New throttle tips for when you open it too fast or take too long to reach full throttle.
- Each lap's line comes from the game's real position, and the corners are numbered by the track's shape.
- Telemetry has a Basic and a Full mode with the speed, throttle, brake, delta and gear traces, and Compare laps live
  against your best, your last lap or another lap, corner by corner.
- Standings show the gap to the leader or the interval, a class filter when several classes race and your position lap
  by lap, and Tyres show their wear.
- Home shows your laps, hours and races of the month, your last race lap by lap and your recent sessions, like the web
  and the PC.
- Leagues are open to everyone.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.5 beta

- Recent records are now cards with the discipline's colour, the time, who set it and the lap it beat. Tap one to
  open its leaderboard, its track page or the driver's profile.

## 0.4.4 beta

- A lap is only invalid if you leave the track or lose the connection, just like in iRacing. Going through the pit
  lane no longer ruins it.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.3 beta

- The app looks new, darker with the amber accent, a floating tab bar and clearer sub-tabs. Home opens on your last
  race, you can run your finger along your iRating on every licence card and in My races, and the Coach tells you
  first how far you are and what to work on, then what each corner costs.
- An offline race against the AI no longer shows up as your licence.
- Laps cut short by a reset or a tow never count as a best lap or a record.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.2 beta

- Your track notes are on each track's page, the same ones as on your PC and the web, and you can write them there
  too. You'll find them all in Account → Track notes.
- Driver profiles show their leaderboards, records and top 3 finishes at a glance, and a recent record opens its
  driver's profile.
- The same car under different badges now shares one leaderboard. That goes for the SF23's Honda and Toyota,
  NASCAR and the Supercars.
- Fixes and improvements.

## 0.4.1 beta

- Every lap your PC records is in your account, with Laps, Coach, My sessions and My races, the lap analysis, the
  map, the braking points and the racing line.
- Follow your race live from the PC with Telemetry, Standings and Tyres, or watch a friend with a share code.
- Leaderboards by car and track for every discipline, recent records, track pages and the leagues.
- Your iRating and licence in every discipline, race summaries and your notes on other drivers.
- One account on every device, and you can link Discord from Account → Discord.
